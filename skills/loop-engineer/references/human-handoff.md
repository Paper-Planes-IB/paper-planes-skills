# Human handoff

Use this order. Omit empty sections.

Apply `/Users/khudovekov/.codex/skills/sergey-response-style/SKILL.md` to the final presentation. Lead with the material outcome or decision. Keep technical evidence last. Preserve enough detail for Sergey to judge the machine's hypotheses and weakest assumption.

## Итерация X из Y

State the purpose of the current iteration and its acceptance criterion.

## Что вы хотели получить

One Job Story in human language: situation, desired progress and value.

## Что получилось

Link the result. State what it enables and its readiness for internal use and client delivery.

## Где машина отклонилась

Name the material gap, affected outcome and confidence. Avoid internal detector vocabulary unless it helps Sergey decide.

## Что машина уже улучшила

List only applied changes with a checked effect. Separate them from proposals.

## Что нужно решить

Ask only when a decision changes scope, permissions, irreversible action, disputed product meaning or global behavior. Provide 2-3 options with meaning and consequence, recommendation, confidence, and what happens after the choice.

## Как проверено мышление

Show the problem representation, important hypotheses with status, evidence and counterevidence, alternatives considered, weakest assumption, blind spot and the condition that would change the recommendation. Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought.

## Доказательства и техническая квитанция

Put task IDs, commits, tests, schemas, logs and receipt links here, last.

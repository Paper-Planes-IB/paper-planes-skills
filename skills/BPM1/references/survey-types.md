# Survey Types

Use this reference after classifying the survey type.

## 1. B2C Choice

Question families:

- screening / category participation;
- purchase and consumption frequency;
- occasions and scenarios;
- choice factors;
- barriers / non-use;
- switching / churn / competitor use;
- brand awareness / consideration / purchase;
- trust and information channels;
- price / WTP;
- open language;
- factor matrix for k-means;
- JTBD bridge.

Typical slides:

- category penetration / behavior;
- factor ranking and criticality;
- barriers;
- segment/cluster map;
- JTBD by cluster;
- price/WTP;
- channel/trust map;
- product/offer implications;
- QFD / voice-to-product;
- storyline evidence gates.

Anti-patterns:

- treating stated importance as actual behavior;
- running k-means on invalid matrix fields;
- interpreting brand awareness as preference;
- overusing `BPM-3` as if B2C always needs interviews.

## 2. EVP / Employer Choice

Question families:

- current role/industry/seniority;
- career reasons;
- job search channels;
- employer choice factors;
- awareness and consideration of employer brands;
- reasons for not considering a company;
- offer/package expectations;
- image attributes by employer;
- demographics and employment context.

Typical slides:

- employer choice factor ranking;
- channel map for hiring;
- employer awareness/consideration funnel;
- competitor employer image;
- EVP gaps;
- talent segment priorities.

Anti-patterns:

- applying B2C NPS logic to employer choice;
- mixing internal employee satisfaction with external employer attractiveness;
- treating general job-market statistics as company-specific perception.

## 3. Internal Employee

Question families:

- department, role, tenure, employee category;
- leadership / manager block;
- rules and decision-making;
- focus on duty vs flexible tasks;
- future orientation;
- culture, communication, engagement;
- benefits and employer support;
- internal barriers and improvement ideas;
- eNPS if used.

Typical slides:

- culture profile;
- manager/leadership diagnostics;
- benefits and support priority map;
- internal segment differences;
- retention and engagement risks;
- organizational change implications.

Anti-patterns:

- comparing eNPS to B2C NPS;
- treating internal survey as external employer market;
- ignoring department/category base sizes.

## 4. Event / Conference

Question families:

- attendance/non-attendance;
- reasons for non-attendance;
- frequency of event participation;
- purpose of attendance;
- ticket purchase logic: self/company;
- online/offline preference;
- WTP for offline/online;
- factor ratings for online/offline events;
- competitor events;
- return intent and recommendation.

Typical slides:

- attendee/non-attendee split;
- event barriers;
- purpose/JTBD of attendance;
- price bands;
- online/offline value drivers;
- offer/package implications;
- acquisition and retention routes.

Anti-patterns:

- mixing professional development goals with entertainment event logic;
- treating one event's NPS as category benchmark;
- ignoring company-paid vs self-paid ticket behavior.

## 5. B2B2C

Question families:

- end-customer demand and choice factors;
- channel/dealer/distributor role;
- service and logistics requirements;
- commercial policy;
- documents, timings, specification influence;
- product and channel barriers separately.

Typical slides:

- dual-sided factor map;
- channel role map;
- service blueprint;
- product vs channel pain split;
- CRM/commercial policy implications.

Anti-patterns:

- mixing channel factors with consumer factors in one prior;
- treating dealer interviews as end-customer survey;
- treating end-customer survey as proof of distributor readiness.
